Shopify Speed Optimization

Why Custom Coding Beats Third-Party Apps: Many Shopify store owners today are struggling with website speed. Your site might look professional, but if it doesn't meet Google's speed requirements, you will lose both search rankings and potential customers.

My Recent Shopify Optimization Project

I recently worked on a Shopify store where the client's top priority was high-speed performance to maximize user experience. I took a "code-first" approach instead of relying on heavy, resource-draining plugins.

The Challenges & My Custom Solutions:

WhatsApp Integration: Instead of installing a heavy app that slows down the site, I implemented a floating WhatsApp button using custom HTML and CSS.

Review Section: The client initially requested a review app, but I explained that these scripts often load before the main site content, causing significant lag. I built a custom, lightweight review section.

Image Optimization: The provided product images were large, unoptimized PNG files. I manually compressed these and converted them to WebP format.

The Result:

After finalizing these technical optimizations, we ran a speed test. The mobile performance score jumped from 42 to 95+, effectively resolving Core Web Vitals and Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) warnings.

Conclusion

If you are struggling with a slow Shopify store, the solution isn't adding more apps—it's cleaning up your code. Let's work together to optimize your store, improve your Google rankings, and provide a seamless experience for your customers.
